# Final Report Compilation Plan Template

## Gate 1 — Front Matter

Create and approve a separate front-matter file containing the institution's
required title page, declaration, certification/approval, dedication,
acknowledgements, abstract, table of contents, list of tables, list of figures,
and abbreviations. Do not infer student, institution, programme, supervisor,
signatory, or date details. Compilation begins only after explicit approval.

## Gate 2 — Integrity Audit

- confirm one approved file for each chapter;
- compare title, problem, aim, objectives, research questions, scope, methods,
  results, conclusions, and recommendations;
- verify metrics, test totals, system boundaries, and safety claims;
- audit source dates and reciprocal citation/reference coverage;
- add missing verified references, remove duplicates/uncited entries, and sort
  unique entries alphabetically;
- normalise table/figure captions;
- decide approved appendices; and
- preserve historical data/context disclosures.

## Gate 3 — Controlled Merge

Create a new report file. Preserve approved sources. Merge front matter,
Chapters One–Five, references, and appendices in institutional order. Use real
section breaks: Roman preliminary pages with hidden title-page number, then
Arabic page 1 at Chapter One continuing through end matter. Start every chapter
on a new page.

## Gate 4 — Navigation and Fields

Use real heading and caption styles. Populate and update the automatic table of
contents, list of tables, list of figures, page fields, caption numbers, and
cross-references. Confirm displayed page numbers match actual chapter starts and
remove field errors or drafting instructions.

## Gate 5 — Whole-Report QA

Check front-matter order and details, page geometry, fonts, black headings,
spacing, hierarchy, pagination, captions, visuals, tables, citations,
references, spelling, acronym coverage, accessibility, privacy, secret removal,
and unsupported claims. Render/inspect every page when tooling permits.

## Gate 6 — Owner Review and Submission

Deliver a review DOCX first. After the owner supplies remaining personal/
institutional details and approves layout, update all fields, perform a final
visual review, and export the submission PDF from the approved DOCX. Preserve
the source chapters and approved front matter for recovery.
